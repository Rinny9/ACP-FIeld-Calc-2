## Limits and clinical review items

This audit establishes relevant source pages and fixes navigation. It does not independently certify every clinical statement, dose, local authorization, or equipment convention.

- Tension-pneumothorax note: the older app summary says 2-inch adult-size equipment and a pediatric site preference; current CD p.69 differs, including a minimum 2.5-inch adult-size catheter. The app now explicitly warns not to rely on its older summary and to check the source/local RBHP guidance.
- Cardiac ischemia: the blanket “do not restart if vital signs recover” summary was not confirmed in current CD pp.27–28 or PCS pp.121–123. Its old citation was removed and an explicit warning added.
- Hypoglycemia: the D50-to-D10 preparation recipe was not found in current CD pp.40–41. PCS p.148 allows dilution but does not specify that recipe. Its old citation was removed and an explicit warning added.
- The “RED RULE” phrase was not found in the current CD; the app labels that retained instruction as a local medication-labelling safety check, not a CD rule.
- Adult resting RR 12–20 is now identified as an external convention; PCS p.14 is retained for its threshold definitions only.

## Navigation checks

Reference links include `#page=` with the verified PDF viewer page. Each page in a list/range is individually linked; mixed PCS and CD citations no longer share one destination. Inline directive references and directive source-page ranges are clickable. Some mobile PDF viewers ignore `#page`; the visible viewer-page label and fallback note allow manual navigation without assuming a specific phone/browser implementation.
